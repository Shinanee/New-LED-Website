Cabinet Type Transparent LED Screens

Cabinet Type Transparent LED Screens are innovative display solutions designed to deliver dynamic visuals while maintaining high levels of transparency. Commonly used in glass facades, retail storefronts, and stage designs, they combine advanced LED technology with a modular cabinet structure for enhanced performance and flexibility.

High Transparency

Offers 60–90% transparency, ideal for glass surfaces. Maintains visibility behind the screen. Perfect for showrooms, malls, and facades. Delivers a clean and open look.

Modular Cabinet Design

Built with standard-size cabinets (e.g., 500×1000mm). Easy to install, replace, and expand. Ensures seamless screen assembly. Great for large customized displays.

Slim and Lightweight

Ultra-thin aluminum structure minimizes load. Easy to hang or mount on glass walls. Saves space while enhancing design. Suitable for indoor and semi-outdoor use.

High Brightness

Brightness up to 6000 nits for daylight visibility. Content remains clear even under sunlight. Adjustable for indoor or outdoor conditions. Enhances advertising impact.
