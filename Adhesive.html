Adhesive Type LED Screen

Ultra-Thin and Lightweight

Adhesive LED screens are extremely slim, often just a few millimeters thick, making them easy to stick on surfaces like glass walls, windows, or acrylic panels.

Transparent Display

They maintain high transparency (up to 70–90%), allowing natural light to pass through, which is ideal for storefronts and glass partitions.

Self-Adhesive Backing

These screens come with a peel-and-stick design, eliminating the need for brackets or heavy structural supports.

Easy Installation

Quick to mount without damaging surfaces — perfect for retail, showrooms, and temporary displays.

Flexible Custom Sizing

Can be cut and joined to fit different screen sizes or window dimensions, making it adaptable to unique layouts.

Lightweight Power Supply Integration

Power and signal lines are thin and discreet, which helps maintain a clean aesthetic.
